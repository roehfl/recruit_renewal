Phase 04b - Admin Interview Schedule Management

Completed Generated: 2026-05-26

Executive Summary

Phase 04b implements administrator interview schedule management on top of the Phase 04a interview domain.

Administrators can list/detail schedules, create and update DRAFT schedules, replace DRAFT participants, confirm eligible schedules, and cancel confirmed schedules.

The implementation reads StageResult only for previous-stage eligibility and does not create, update, announce, correct, or publish stage results.

The phase can move forward to applicant-owned interview schedule reads in Phase 04c.

Validation And Policy

Stage Rules

  • Allowed stage types: FIRST_INTERVIEW, SECOND_INTERVIEW, FINAL_INTERVIEW.
  • Create/update/replace/confirm require READY or IN_PROGRESS.
  • RESULT_ANNOUNCED and CLOSED reject mutable commands.
  • Cancel is allowed only when the stage is READY or IN_PROGRESS.
  • RESULT_ANNOUNCED and CLOSED reject cancel.

Participant Rules

  • Replace request lists cannot be null.
  • DRAFT can have empty participant lists.
  • Confirm requires at least one candidate and one interviewer.
  • Duplicate IDs and duplicate non-null sort orders are rejected per role.
  • Candidate application must be same posting and SUBMITTED.

Eligibility And Collision

  • Previous stage must be RESULT_ANNOUNCED or CLOSED.
  • Previous-stage result must exist and be PASSED.
  • Confirmed schedule collisions are checked by same candidate application or same employee.
  • Cancelled schedules are ignored by collision checks.
