Domain Structure

Entity Relationships

JobPosting 1 - N Interview
Stage      1 - N Interview

Interview 1 - N InterviewParticipant

InterviewParticipant(CANDIDATE)   N - 1 JobApplication
InterviewParticipant(INTERVIEWER) N - 1 Employee

Ownership Rules

  • Interview belongs to one posting and one stage.
  • Stage.jobPosting must match Interview.jobPosting.
  • Candidate rows require JobApplication and no Employee.
  • Interviewer rows require Employee and no JobApplication.
  • Participant rows do not reference User directly.
